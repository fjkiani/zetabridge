## Research purpose
Resolve our bulk transcriptomic immune composite into single-cell cell-type fractions. This dataset has matched MSI and MSS arms (treatment-naïve and post-treatment) with documented differences in CD8+ T memory/effector cells, B cells, and Tregs — directly paralleling our TCGA finding that MSI-H tumors have elevated immune infiltrate vs MSS. We will recompute the composite at the cell-type-proportion level and test responder vs progressor separation.
