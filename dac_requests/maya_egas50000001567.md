## Research purpose (non-commercial, methods validation)
We are validating a transcriptomic immune-infiltrate composite (CD8A/CD3D/CD68 + CD274/PDCD1/FOXP3) as a **predictive** biomarker of response to immune-checkpoint blockade in microsatellite-stable (MSS) metastatic colorectal cancer. The MAYA trial is the only published MSS mCRC cohort with spatial immune profiling linked to ICI response. We will (a) recompute the composite from raw RNA-seq, (b) test its association with objective response and the published spatial CD8+KI67+/CD68+CD163+ predictors, and (c) benchmark against the published finding that post-TMZ TMB increase associates with PFS. No commercial use; results for methods validation and citation.
