## Research purpose
Cross-validate whether the BM1/BM2 immune subtypes from this BRAF V600E trial recapitulate the CMS1 immune-hot signature our composite uses as a reference frame, and test the published finding that on-treatment tumor cell-intrinsic immune-program induction (not pre-treatment TMB/CMS/BM1-BM2) correlates with outcome. Our composite genes (PDCD1, CD8A, CD274, FOXP3) are all in scope.
